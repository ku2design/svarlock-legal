8. Media vault (Pro)

The media vault lets you hide photos, videos, and other files inside SvarLock. When you hide an item, SvarLock copies it into the app's private storage on your phone, encrypts it, and then removes the original from your gallery or file manager. Please read the following carefully.

  • Hidden items exist only inside SvarLock, on this phone. They are not uploaded to any server, are not included in Android or cloud backups, and do not move to a new phone. We have no copy of them and no way to recover them.
  • Uninstalling SvarLock deletes your hidden items permanently. The same happens if you clear SvarLock's data, reset your phone, or lose or damage the phone. Unhide any item you want to keep before you do any of these things.
  • SvarLock is not responsible for the loss, deletion, or corruption of any file while you use the media vault. This includes loss caused by uninstalling the app, clearing its data, a forgotten PIN, a device fault, a storage error, low storage space, an operating-system update, or an interruption while SvarLock hides, unhides, or deletes an item.
  • Deleting an item from the vault removes it permanently. It cannot be undone.
  • The vault is not a backup service. Keep your own separate copy of any file that matters to you.
